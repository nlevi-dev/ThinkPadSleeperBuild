# Candidates

← [Back to README](../README.md)

The T60 chassis puts a hard cap on what can physically fit. The 14 inch model allows up to **311 x 255 mm** and the 15 inch up to **329 x 268 mm**. Anything wider or deeper won't close.

The only realistic candidates with a discrete GPU that fit the 14 inch are the **ROG Flow X13** variants. The 15 inch opens it up to the **Razer Blade 14** and **ROG Zephyrus G14** as well.

Each model has multiple SKUs — one row per model showing the top GPU tier available.
